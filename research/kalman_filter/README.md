# Reconstruction of long-term complex system dynamics from short-term fragments of observational records
The materials for the article consist of 2 notebooks for numerical experiments (A, B) and 1 python script for series generation (C) and data from dplR analysis (D).

A) research/kalman_filter/reconstruction_of_long_term_complex_system_dynamics/synthetic_benchmarks.ipynb for *Synthetic benchmarks* subsection.

B) research/kalman_filter/reconstruction_of_long_term_complex_system_dynamics/dendrochronological_series.ipynb for *Application to fragmented dendrochronological time series* subsection.

C) research/kalman_filter/reconstruction_of_long_term_complex_system_dynamics/gen_filter_save.py for *Generating synthetic data*.

D) research/kalman_filter/reconstruction_of_long_term_complex_system_dynamics/dplR.

# The Impact of Kalman Filter Transition Model Selection on the Effectiveness of Long-Term Correlated Time Series Reconstruction
Materials for the PIERE 2026 paper consist of 1 notebook for numerical experiments (A) and 1 python script for series generation and filtering (B).

A) research/kalman_filter/the_impact_of_kalman_filter_transition_model_selection/the_impact_of_kalman_filter_transition_model_selection.ipynb: signal plots, Pearson correlation / MSE and ACF comparisons, and the transition matrices in numerical form.

B) research/kalman_filter/reconstruction_of_long_term_complex_system_dynamics/gen_filter_save.py: shared with the article above. It builds the transition matrix F for the `YW` (Yule-Walker AR estimate) and `Baseline` (kinematic constant-velocity model).
