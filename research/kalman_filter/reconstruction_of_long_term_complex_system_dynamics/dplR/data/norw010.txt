Measurement type:  Total ring width

Detrend Method:
Negative exponontial or linear regression with negative or 0 slope

Location of core material:
University of Tromsoe, Department of Biology, N-9037 Tromsoe, Norway


Reference:
Published back to AD 1358 (EPS 85 criterion) in:

Kirchhefer, A.J. 2001 Reconstruction of summer temperatures from tree-
rings of Scots pine (Pinus sylvestris L.) in coastal northern Norway.
The Holocene 11(1), 41-52.


Site information:
- slope 15°W, moraine ridge, glacifluvial sediments, talus slope, open-
canopy tree-line habitat (arctic-alpine-atlantic), living trees and
stumps, sampling area 0.5 km * 0.2 km
Elevation Range 50-170m.
- potential growth release due to logging in the 17th century.

Please note, site Forfjorddalen (FORFJ1) will be contributed to the ITRDB
following publication.


Measuring type: the first measurements with 0.01mm resolution
(Tucson M-format), later 0.001 mm (Tucson L-format).


Missing rings coding:
In the 0.01mm-format, missing rings are zero.
Others, internal coding with:
        - zero: "not sure where the ring belongs exactly",
        - 0.001: "very likely that it belongs here",
        - 0.005: some morphological evidence for the missing ring
        - 0.010: wedging ring
        - 0.020: smallest measurable complete ring (one cell layer)
